Good to know

Quick answers before you order.

How long does made to measure take?

About 14 days from when you place the order, plus delivery time to your city.

Do you ship outside Pakistan?

Yes. Switch to USD at the top of the page, and we ship worldwide with tracking.

What if the size isn't right?

Ready-to-wear pieces can be exchanged within 7 days. Use Find my size to get it right the first time.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery in Pakistan, or bank transfer and card for international orders.
